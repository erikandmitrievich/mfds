#+begin_quote
(Closed expression for the Fibonacci sequences)

We are well aware of the recursive definition of a Fibonacci sequence (see ex. 1.32). In the same exercise, we have also solved for the closed form of expression for the $n$ -th term of the standard Fibonacci sequence $(0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, \ldots)$. We shall now try and obtain the closed expression for any Fibonacci sequence.

(i) Let $x = (x_i)_{i=0}^{\infty}$ be a real Fibonacci sequence. Determine the matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{2\times 2}$ such that

$$\begin{pmatrix} x_{n+1} \\ x_n \end{pmatrix} = A \begin{pmatrix} x_n \\ x_{n-1} \end{pmatrix}, \quad \forall n \in \mathbb{N}$$

(ii) Obtain an eigenbasis for $A$ and therefore diagonalize $A$.
