** Partial-credit protocol

For an S5 theorem you didn't prepare. At most 20 min on the first pass, then come back at the end.

1. Do (a) completely; the algorithm and definitions are recall.
2. State the theorem precisely, with its hypotheses.
3. Name the technique and write its outline: exchange argument (greedy), cut-and-paste (DP), indicator variables + linearity (expected time), decision-tree counting (lower bounds), induction on height or size (trees), loop invariant (iterative algorithms), recurrence + master theorem (divide and conquer).
4. State the lemmas it needs, even if you can't prove them.
5. Prove the easy pieces: base case, trivial bound, one direction.
