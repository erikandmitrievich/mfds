** Revised strategic fork

The old 32-point target sat between two boundaries and was served by a balanced allocation. The real boundaries are 30 and 35, and they pull in opposite directions. The current plan serves neither.

*** Branch A — target grade 1 (≥ 35)

Requires S3 ∧ S4 ∧ S5. S5 has the highest elasticity: $\partial P(1)/\partial p_5 = 0.68$ against $0.36$ for $p_3$ and $0.38$ for $p_4$. *** Branch B — target a secure 2 (≥ 30, ideally 32 for margin)

** DECISION RULES

*** Behind — trigger conditions, not feelings

You are behind if *any* of these fires:
- The 23 Sept re-sit scores more than 6 points below the 12 Sept baseline. Meaning: the gap protocol failed and Block 2 is now relearning rather than buying coverage.
- Mock A on 26 Sept scores under 28/40.

- Any single timed pseudocode procedure still exceeds 5 minutes on Day 10.

*** Cut order if a day is lost

1. *First:* the timed pseudocode passes (Day 6 and Day 7 blocks reduced to one pass each, Day 10's dropped). Throughput insurance, not coverage.
2. *Second:* the Huffman encoding on Day 9. It is the verbatim branch only.
3. *Third:* Thm 11.2 on Day 8. It shares its skeleton with Thm 7.4, so it is the cheapest proof to lose and the most redundant.

Never cut, in any circumstance: the exam-rule card, the recurrence engine, the invariant machine on two instances, the 12 Sept baseline, the gap artefact, Mock A.

*** Branch switch

If two or more triggers fire by the end of Day 8, switch to Branch B for Days 9–11: drop Thm 11.2 and BUILD-MAX-HEAP from the rep schedule entirely, move those ~2.5 h into repair and the structure matrix, and accept a secure 2. The switch is only available on Day 8 or 9 — after Mock B it is too late to convert hours into breadth.

*** Minimum floor

Complete even if everything else collapses: the exam-rule card; the recurrence engine with the three case conditions; the invariant machine on two instances; the four-cell matrix for heaps, red-black and hash tables; one S5 proof reproducible cold. That subset scores somewhere between 21 and 29.5 depending on the rotation — a 4 at worst, a 3 at best. It is not a target, it is the thing that stops a collapse from becoming a fifth attempt.
