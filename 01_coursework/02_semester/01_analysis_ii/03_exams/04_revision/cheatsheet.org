* Affine linear approximation

$$L_f(x) = f(x_0) + \nabla f(x_0)^{\top}(x - x_0)$$

* Length of the curve

$$L(\gamma) = \int_0^1 \|\gamma'(t)\|dt$$

* Convex function definition

#+begin_quote
A function $f : \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ is called a convex iff for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ and all $t \in [0,1]$,

$$f(tx + (1 - t)y) \le tf(x) + (1 - t)f(y)$$
#+end_quote

* Taylor with Jacobian

$$f(x) = f(x_0) + (\nabla f(x_0))^\top (x - x_0) + \frac{1}{2}(x - x_0)^\top H_f(x_0)(x - x_0) + o(\|x - x_0\|^2)$$

* Gradient descent

$$x_{k+1} = x_k - n \nabla f(x_k)$$

* Prove submanifold

1. Find $g : U \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^r$ with $M \cap U = \{g(x) = 0\} = M$, $r = n-k$.

2. Chech $g \in \mathcal{C}^1$.

3. Take $U$.

4. Verify $M \cap U = M$.

5. Compute $g'(x)$.

6. Show $\mathrm{rank}(g'(x)) = r$ for all $x \in M \cap U$.

* Tangent and normal spaces

\begin{align*}
T_x(M) &= \{\nabla g_1(x), \ldots, \nabla g_r(x)\}^\perp, \\
N_x(M) &= \operatorname{span}\{\nabla g_1(x), \ldots, \nabla g_r(x)\}.
\end{align*}

* Newton step

\begin{align*}
x_{k+1} &= x_k - \frac{f(x_k)}{f'(x_k)} \\
x_{k+1} &= x_k - \nabla f(x_k)^{-1} f(x_k) \\
\end{align*}

* Inverse of $2 \times 2$ matrix

\begin{align*}
A &=
\begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{pmatrix} \\
\det(A) &= ad - bc \\
A^{-1} &= \frac{1}{\det(A)}
\begin{pmatrix}
d & -b \\
-c & a
\end{pmatrix} \\
\end{align*}

* Quick linearly and quadratically convergent sequences

\begin{align*}
c &\in (0,1) \\
x_{k+1} &= c^{k} \quad (\text{linear}) \\
x_{k+1} &= c^{2^{k}} \quad (\text{quadratic}) \\
\end{align*}

* Newton iteration on $Ax - b = 0$

\begin{align*}
x_{k+1} &= x_k - A^{-1}(Ax_k - b) \\
        &= x_k - (A^{-1}Ax_k - A^{-1}b) \\
        &= x_k - (x_k - A^{-1}b) \\
        &= A^{-1}b \\
\end{align*}

Newton converges in one step.

* $\|Ax - b\|^2$

Tricks:

- $b^{\top}Ax = x^{\top}A^{\top}b$ (if its a constant)

- $DL(x)[h] = \langle \nabla L(x), h \rangle = (\nabla L(x))^{\top}h$

* Banach fixed point theorem

Let $(X, d)$ be a complete metric space and let $f : X \rightarrow X$ be a contraction.

$f$ is a contraction iff $\exists q \in [0,1)$ such that

$$d(f(x), f(y)) \le gd(x, y) \quad \forall x, y \in X$$

Then,

1. $f$ has a unique fixed point $x \in X : f(x) = x$.

2. For every $x_0 \in X,\ x_{k+1} = f(x_n)$ converges linearly to the fixed point $x$.

3. One has the approximation estimates, $\forall n \ge 0$,

   $$d(x_n, x) \le \dfrac{q^n}{1 - q} d(x_1, x_0)$$

* Constrained 2nd order rule

1. Construct Lagrange ($L(x, \lambda) = f(x) - \lambda g(x)$).

2. Find critical points.

3. Find tangent space to restriction derivative $T_x = \ker(g'(x))$.

4. Choose vector based on $T_x$.

5. Assess critical point on $v^{\top}H_xLv$. ($>0 \Rightarrow \text{min},\ <0 \Rightarrow \text{max}$)


* Lagrange multipliers justification

"Since $\nabla g(x) \neq 0$ on $M = \{g = 0\}$, Lagrange multipliers corrollary gives that every constrained extremum satisfies $\nabla f(x) = \lambda \nabla g(x)$ for some $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$."
