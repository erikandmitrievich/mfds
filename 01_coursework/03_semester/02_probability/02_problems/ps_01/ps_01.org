* Problem 1 :subspace:
** Task
#+begin_quote
*Conceptual.* Take $\Omega = \mathbb{R}$, where an outcome $\omega$ is a temperature measured in degrees Celsius. A sensor answers only the two questions

\[ A = \{ \omega \in \Omega \mid \omega < 18 \}, \quad B = \{ \omega \in \Omega \mid \omega < 22 \} \]

Find a sigma-algebra containing $A$ and $B$ with as few sets as possible, and justify why no smaller sigma-algebra can work. Is $\{20\}$ an event in this sigma-algebra?
#+end_quote

#+begin_quote

\[ \mathbb{P}(A \setminus B) = \mathbb{P}(A) - \mathbb{P}(A \cap B) \]

and use it to derive the two-event union formula

\[ \mathbb{P}(A \cup B) = \mathbb{P}(A) + \mathbb{P}(B) - \mathbb{P}(A \cap B) \]
