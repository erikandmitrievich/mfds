* Item 1 - the exam-rule card

** Loop-termination

For every loop-invariant proof, write that loop *terminates* and the state of its invariant at termination. Initialisation -> maintenance -> termination.

** No $O/\Theta$ in an induction hypothesis

In induction hypothesis write $t(n) \le c n \lg(n)$ with an *explicit named constant*, never $t(n) = O(n\lg(n))$. The reason is that in Landau notation, constant is *allowed to change* in each at every level of recursion, so the "proof" proves nothing.

** Watershed procedure

For any $T(n) = aT(\dfrac{n}{b}) + f(n)$, extract $a$ and $b$, and compute $w(n) = n^{\log_b(a)}$, then compare.

** $\mathbb{Z}_p$ field caveat

In a field, only nonzero elements have multiplicative inverses, an $1 \neq 0$ (so a field has at least 2 elements).

* Item 2 - encode master theorem verbatim

*Master Theorem*

Let $a > 0, b > 1$ be constants. Let $f(n) \ge 0$ be the driving function. Define recurrance $T(n)$ on $n \in \mathbb{N}$ by:

$$T(n) = a T(\frac{n}{b}) + f(n)$$

such that:

$$aT(\frac{n}{b}) = a'T(\lfloor \frac{n}{b} \rfloor) + a''T(\lceil \frac{n}{b} \rceil)\ :\ a', a'' \ge 0,\ a = a' + a''$$

1. If $\exists \epsilon > 0 : f(n) = O(n^{\log_b(a - \epsilon)})$, then $T(n) = \Theta(n^{\log_b(a)})$

2. If $\exists k \ge 0 : f(n) = \Theta(n^{\log_b(a)} \lg^{k}(n))$, then $T(n) = \Theta(n^{\log_b(a)} \lg^{k+1}(n))$

3. If $\exists \epsilon > 0 : f(n) = \Omega(n^{\log_b(a + \epsilon)})$, and $af(\frac{n}{b}) < cf(n)$ for $c < 1$ and $\forall$ sufficienlty large $n$, then $T(n) = \Theta(f(n))$

* Item 3 - watershed drill

** Procedure

Procedure to drill:

1. Extract $a, b, f(n)$.

2. Compute $\log_b(a)$, write $w(n) = n^{\log_b(a)}$.

3. Form the ratio $\dfrac{f(n)}{w(n)}$ and name its shape.

4. Classify from the ratio:

   - $O(n^{-\epsilon}) \rightarrow \text{case 1}$

   - $\Theta(\lg^kn) \text{ with } k \ge 0 \rightarrow \text{case 2}$

   - $\Omega(n^{\epsilon}) \rightarrow \text{case 3}$, then check regularity ($af(\frac{n}{b}) < cf(n),\ c < 1$)

   - Anything else $\rightarrow$ master theorem does not apply

5. Write the bound: $\Theta(w)$, $\Theta(w \lg^{k+1}n)$, $\Theta(f)$

** Drill

*** $T(n) = 16\,T\!\left(\frac{n}{4}\right) + n^2$

\begin{align*}
a &= 16,\ b = 4,\ f(n) = n^2 \\
\implies w(n) &= n^{\log_b(a)} \\
&= n^{\log_{4}(16)} \\
&= n^2 \\
\therefore \frac{f(n)}{w(n)} &= \frac{n^2}{n^2} \\
&= 1 = \lg^{k}n : k = 0 \rightarrow \text{case 2} \\
\therefore T(n) &= \Theta(n^2 \lg(n))
\end{align*}


*** $T(n) = 3\,T\!\left(\frac{n}{2}\right) + \frac{n}{\lg(n)}$

\begin{align*}
a &= 3,\ b = 2,\ f(n) = \frac{n}{\lg(n)} \\
w(n) &= n^{\log_2(3)} \\
\therefore \frac{f(n)}{w(n)} &= \frac{\frac{n}{\lg(n)}}{n^{\log_2(3)}} \\
&= \frac{n}{n^{\log_2(3)}\lg(n)} \quad (1 < \log_2(3) < 2) \\
&= \frac{1}{n^{\log_2(3) - 1}\lg(n)} \rightarrow \text{case 1} \\
\therefore T(n) &= \Theta(n^{\log_2(3)})
\end{align*}



*** $T(n) = 4\,T\!\left(\frac{n}{2}\right) + n^2 \lg n$

\begin{align*}
a &= 4,\ b = 2,\ f(n) = n^2 \lg(n) \\
w(n) &= n^{\log_2(4)} \\
&= n^{2} \\
\therefore \frac{f(n)}{w(n)} &= \frac{n^2 \lg(n)}{n^2} \\
&= \lg(n) \rightarrow \text{case 2} \\
\therefore T(n) &= \Theta(n^2 \lg^2(n))
\end{align*}


*** $T(n) = 2\,T\!\left(\frac{n}{4}\right) + \sqrt{n}$

\begin{align*}
a &= 2,\ b = 4,\ f(n) = \sqrt{n} \\
w(n) &= n^{\log_4(2)} \\
&= n^{1/2} \\
\therefore \frac{f(n)}{w(n)} &= \frac{n^{1/2}}{n^{1/2}} \\
&= 1 \rightarrow \text{case 2} \\
\therefore T(n) &= \Theta(n^{1/2} \lg(n))
\end{align*}

*** $T(n) = 2T(\frac{n}{4}) + n$

\begin{align*}
a &= 2,\ b = 4,\ f(n) = n \\
w(n) &= n^{1/2} \\
\therefore \frac{f(n)}{w(n)} &= \frac{n}{n^{1/2}} \\
&= n^{1/2} \\
\implies af(\frac{n}{b}) &< cf(n) : c < 1\ \text{for some}\ n \\
2\frac{n}{4} &< cn \\
\frac{n}{2} &< cn \quad \text{choose}\ 0,5 < c < 1 \\
\therefore T(n) &= \Theta(n)
\end{align*}


*** $T(n) = 5\,T\!\left(\frac{n}{2}\right) + n^3$

\begin{align*}
a &= 5,\ b = 2,\ f(n) = n^3 \\
w(n) &= n^{\log_2(5)} \quad (2 < \log_2(5) < 3) \\
\therefore \frac{f(n)}{w(n)} &= \frac{n^3}{n^{\log_2(5)}} \\
&= n^{3 - \log_2(5)} \\
\implies af(\frac{n}{b}) &< cf(n) : c < 1, \text{for some}\ n \\
5(\frac{n}{2})^3 &< cn^3 \\
\frac{5}{8}n^3 &< cn^3 \quad \text{choose}\ 5/8 < c < 1 \\
\therefore T(n) &= \Theta(n^3)
\end{align*}


*** $T(n) = T\!\left(\frac{n}{2}\right) + \lg(n)$

\begin{align*}
a &= 1,\ b = 2,\ f(n) = \lg(n) \\
w(n) &= n^0 \\
&= 1 \\
\therefore \frac{f(n)}{w(n)} &= \lg(n) \\
&= \lg(n) = \lg^{k}(n) : k = 1 \rightarrow \text{case 2} \\
\therefore T(n) &= \Theta(\lg^2(n))
\end{align*}



*** $T(n) = 2\,T\!\left(\frac{n}{2}\right) + \frac{n}{\lg(n)}$

\begin{align*}
a &= 2,\ b = 2,\ f(n) = \frac{n}{\lg(n)} \\
w(n) &= n \\
\therefore \frac{f(n)}{w(n)} &= \frac{\frac{n}{\lg(n)}}{n} \\
&= \frac{1}{\lg(n)} \\
\therefore\ &\text{master theorem is non applicable}
\end{align*}

* Item 4 - gap recognition + polynomial growth condition

Define $r(n) = \dfrac{f(n)}{w(n)}$.

- $r(n) = O(n^{-\epsilon}) : \epsilon > 0 \rightarrow \text{case 1},\ T(n) = \Theta(w)$

- $r(n) = \Theta(\lg^kn) : k \ge 0 \rightarrow \text{case 2},\ T(n) = \Theta(w \lg^{k+1}n)$

- $r(n) = \Omega(n^{\epsilon}) : \epsilon > 0 \text{ + regularity} \rightarrow \text{case 3},\ T(n) = \Theta(f)$

- $r(n) = \Omega(n^{\epsilon}) : \epsilon > 0 \text{ + regularity fails} \rightarrow \text{no case}$

- $\text{shrinks slower than any}\ n^{-\epsilon} \rightarrow \text{no case}$

- $\text{grows faster than any}\ \lg^k(n), \text{slower than any}\ n^{\epsilon} \rightarrow \text{no case}$

"$\dfrac{f(n)}{w(n)} = \ldots$, which is neither $O(n^{-\epsilon})$ nor $\Theta(\lg^kn)$ for $k \ge 0$. The master theorem does not apply."

$$\exists n_0 \in \mathbb{N}:\ \forall \varphi \ge 1\ \exists d > 1:\ \forall n \ge n_0, \forall \psi \in [1, \varphi]:\ \frac{f(n)}{d} \le f(\psi n) \le d f(n)$$
