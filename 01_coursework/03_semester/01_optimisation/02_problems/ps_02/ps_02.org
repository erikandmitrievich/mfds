* Problem 1 :floating_point:machine_numbers:error_analysis:
** Task
#+begin_quote
Let $\eta = r_{64} \, \mathrm{eps}_{64} = 2^{-1074}$.

(a) Show that

\[ \lvert \mathrm{fl}_{64}(x) - x \rvert \leq \frac{\eta}{2}, \qquad 0 < x < r_{64} \]

(b) Determine $\mathrm{fl}_{64}(x)$ and the relative error for $x = \frac{3}{2}\eta$ and $x = \frac{1}{2}\eta$.

(c) Check your results in Python.
#+end_quote

** Solution

*** (a)

\begin{align*}
0.f \cdot 2^{-1022} &= k \cdot 2^{-52} \cdot 2^{-1022} = k\eta, \quad k = 0, \dots, 2^{52}-1 && \text{(subnormals and } 0\text{)} \\
r_{64} &= 2^{-1022} = 2^{52}\eta \\
&\implies \mathbb{F}_{64} \cap [0, r_{64}] = \{ k\eta : k = 0, \dots, 2^{52} \} \\
x \in (0, r_{64}) &\implies x \in [k\eta, (k+1)\eta] \text{ for some } k \in \{0, \dots, 2^{52}-1\} \\
&\implies \lvert \mathrm{fl}_{64}(x) - x \rvert \leq \frac{\eta}{2} && \text{(round to nearest)}
\end{align*}

*** (b)

\begin{align*}
x_1 &= \frac{3}{2}\eta && \text{(midpoint of } \eta, 2\eta\text{)} \\
\mathrm{fl}_{64}(x_1) &= 2\eta && \text{(tie, round to even: } k = 2\text{)} \\
\varepsilon_{\text{rel}} &= \frac{\lvert 2\eta - \frac{3}{2}\eta \rvert}{\frac{3}{2}\eta} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}\eta}{\frac{3}{2}\eta} = \frac{1}{3}
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
x_2 &= \frac{1}{2}\eta && \text{(midpoint of } 0, \eta\text{)} \\
\mathrm{fl}_{64}(x_2) &= 0 && \text{(tie, round to even: } k = 0\text{)} \\
\varepsilon_{\text{rel}} &= \frac{\lvert 0 - \frac{1}{2}\eta \rvert}{\frac{1}{2}\eta} = 1
\end{align*}

*** (c)
