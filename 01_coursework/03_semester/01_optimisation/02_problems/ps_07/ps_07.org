A_2&: \; 1 \not> 1 \quad (i = 2) \\
A_3&: \; 1 \not> 1 \quad (i = 1)
\end{align*}

Thus,

\[ A_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} \]

\[ x^{(k+1)} = -D^{-1}(L+U)\,x^{(k)}, \quad L+U = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = P \]

\[ \det A_i \neq 0 \;\Rightarrow\; x^* = 0, \quad \mathbf{1} = (1,1,1)^\top = x^{(0)}, \quad P\mathbf{1} = \mathbf{1} \]
