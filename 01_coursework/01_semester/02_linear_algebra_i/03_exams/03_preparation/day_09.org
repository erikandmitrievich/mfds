* Block A: Active Computation (Warm-up)
** Task
#+begin_quote
Let
$$A=\begin{bmatrix}3&1&1\\ 6&3&4\\ 3&1&5\end{bmatrix}\in\mathbb{R}^{3\times3}, \quad b^{(1)}=\begin{bmatrix}1\\ 2\\ 3\end{bmatrix} \quad b^{(2)}=\begin{bmatrix}2\\ 0\\ 1\end{bmatrix}$$

(a) Compute an LU factorization with pivoting, i.e. find $P$, $L$, $U$ such that $PA=LU$.

(b) Using the same $P$, $L$, $U$, solve $Ax=b^{(1)}$ and $Ax=b^{(2)}$. Show the two solutions.
#+end_quote

** Solution

*** (a)

\begin{align*}
P_1\left[ A \mid b^{(1)} \right] &=
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 1 & 0 \\
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1 \\
\end{bmatrix}
\left[ \begin{array}{ccc|c}
3 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\
6 & 3 & 4 & 2 \\
3 & 1 & 5 & 3 \\
\end{array} \right] \\
&=
\left[ \begin{array}{ccc|c}
6 & 3 & 4 & 2 \\
3 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\
3 & 1 & 5 & 3 \\
\end{array} \right]
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
m_{21} &= \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2} \\
(2, 2) &= 1 - (\frac{1}{2})(3) = \frac{2}{2} - \frac{3}{2} = -\frac{1}{2} \\
(2, 3) &= 1 - (\frac{1}{2})(4) = 1 - 2 = -1 \\
(2, 4) &= 1 - (\frac{1}{2})(2) = 0 \\
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
\implies
\left[ \begin{array}{ccc|c}
6 & 3            & 4  & 2 \\
0 & -\frac{1}{2} & -1 & 0 \\
3 & 1            & 5  & 3 \\
\end{array} \right]
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
m_{31} &= \frac{1}{2} \\
(3, 2) &= 1 - (\frac{1}{2})(3) = \frac{2}{2} - \frac{3}{2} = -\frac{1}{2} \\
(3, 3) &= 5 - (\frac{1}{2})(4) = 5 - 2 = 3 \\
(3, 4) &= 3 - (\frac{1}{2})(2) = 3 - 1 = 2 \\
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
\implies
\left[ \begin{array}{ccc|c}
6 & 3            & 4  & 2 \\
0 & -\frac{1}{2} & -1 & 0 \\
0 & -\frac{1}{2} & 3  & 2 \\
\end{array} \right]
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
m_{32} &= 1 \\
(3, 3) &= 3 - (1)(-1) = 4 \\
(3, 4) &= 2 - (1)(0) = 2 \\
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
\implies
\left[ \begin{array}{ccc|c}
6 & 3            & 4  & 2 \\
0 & -\frac{1}{2} & -1 & 0 \\
0 & 0            & 4  & 2 \\
\end{array} \right]
\end{align*}

Hence,

\begin{align*}
P &=
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 1 & 0 \\
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1 \\
\end{bmatrix} \\
L &=
\begin{bmatrix}
1           & 0 & 0 \\
\frac{1}{2} & 1 & 0 \\
\frac{1}{2} & 1 & 1 \\
\end{bmatrix} \\
U &=
\begin{bmatrix}
6 & 3            & 4  \\
0 & -\frac{1}{2} & -1 \\
0 & 0            & 4  \\
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align*}

*** (b)

- $b^{(1)}$

  \begin{align*}
  \left[ \begin{array}{ccc|c}
  6 & 3            & 4  & 2 \\
  0 & -\frac{1}{2} & -1 & 0 \\
  0 & 0            & 4  & 2 \\
  \end{array} \right]
  \end{align*}


  \begin{align*}
  (4)x_3 &= 2 &\implies x_3 &= \frac{1}{2} \\
  (-\frac{1}{2})x_2 + (-1)x_3 &= 0 &\implies x_2 &= -1 \\
  (6)x_1 + (3)x_2 + (4)x_3 &= 2 &\implies x_1 &= \frac{1}{2} \\
  \end{align*}
