* Fr1

** Loop-termination

For every loop-invariant proof, state that loop *terminates*, and the invariant (at termination). Initialisation -> maintainance -> termination.

** No $O/\Theta$ in an induction hypothesis

In induction hypothests write $t(n) \leq cn\lg(n)$ with an *explicit named constant*, never $t(n) \leq O(n\lg(n))$. The reason is that in Landau notation, constant is allowed to change, in each at every level of recursion, so the *proof* proves nothing.

** Watershed procedure

For any $T(n) = aT(\dfrac{n}{b}) + f(n)$, extract $a$ and $b$, and compute $w(n) = n^{\log_{b}(a)}$, then compare.

** $\mathbb{Z}_{p}$ field caveat

In a field, only nonzero elements have multiplicative inverses, an $1 \neq 0$ (so a field has at least 2 elements).
